Serving New Orleans and all of Southern Louisiana

Juneau Odenwald Roofing provides industrial roofing services throughout New Orleans and all of southern Louisiana. Additionally, we regularly complete projects throughout the Gulf Coast region including Louisiana.

Installation, Repair, Replacement, Restoration & Maintenance

Whether you are installing a new industrial roof or repairing and maintaining an older roof, Juneau Odenwald Roofing has the experience and expertise to provide your building with years of roofing life.

We work with all types of industrial roofs including:

  • Built up roofs – coal tar and asphalt
  • Single ply
  • Modified bitumen
  • Metal roof assemblies
  • Solvent and water borne coatings
  • Restoration systems and cements
  • Bitumous coatings
  • Liquid applied membranes
  • Slate roofing

Roof Replacement

The reality for any industrial building is that sooner or later, the roof will need major repairs that are sometimes better solved by a new roof. Our experience in all types of industrial roofing systems secures our ability to guide you to the best possible roofing solution for your particular situation. We can and will guide you to the best long-term response to your roofing needs.
