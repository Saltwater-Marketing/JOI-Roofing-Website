What is a Standing Seam Metal Roof?

Unlike corrugated metal roofs, in which pieces are held together with thousands of screws, a standing seam metal roof is a more modern style of vertical sheet metal panels that are clipped or snapped together.

The clean lines of the raised seams are typically placed vertically, resulting in a beautiful roof design for any structure.

Fewer Screws Means a Reduced Chance of Moisture Leakage

While standing seam metal roofs do have screws to hold the clips that fasten the panels in place, they are hidden from view and are significantly smaller in number compared to the amount of screws used on a corrugated metal roof.

This is important, because the expose screws holes of a corrugated metal roof can get smaller or larger over the years due to weather conditions, making it more likely that it will have moisture leaks compared to a more robust standing seam metal roof.
