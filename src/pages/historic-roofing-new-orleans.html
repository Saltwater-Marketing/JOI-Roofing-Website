Our Historical Roof Renovation Experience

A historic roof is more than your average roof. It plays a significant role in the building's character and historical significance. The roof also defines the style and contributes to the building's beauty.

With historic roofs, few of them withstand the test of time. In general, a roof is a highly vulnerable building element. Because of that vulnerability, homeowners and commercial building companies continue to work so hard to prevent those buildings' deterioration.

And it is no different with historical homes and buildings.

At Juneau Odenwald Roofing, we understand this significance.

We know what it takes to repair and restore a historic building's roof. We even add certain flairs and modifications to ensure their durability and maintenance for years to come.

Our roofing services for historic homes and commercial buildings have allowed us to work on several remarkable properties with historic metal roofing, including the prominent 19th century St. Roch Market.

Working With Us For Your Historical Roof Renovation

Put simply, a poor roof will permit the accelerated deterioration of historic building materials – masonry, wood, plaster, paint – and will cause general disintegration of the building.

We notice that the owners of these historic buildings want to preserve that piece of history, given their uniqueness. A historical roof renovation takes more than the average roof repair.

We are fully aware of this, and this awareness definitely helps us to navigate historical roof renovations in more detail. We provide comprehensive consultation and inspection to adequately address your needs in the best ways possible for your historical roof renovation.

From the shingles to the gutters, we like to see it all. And whether the causes were from water damage to structural damage to termites, we make sure to fully assess the damages to improve your roof from a structural and durability standpoint.

Marrying Preservation and Change in Historic Roof Renovations

We will install your roof with new tweaks to give the building longevity, whatever the case may be.

Our past roof renovations have included various metal roofing styles. For example, with our project for St. Roch Market, we used Galvalume – a material made of galvanized steel with a metal alloy finish - for its historic renovation.

Thanks to our sheet metal fabrication team at Crescent City Copper, we even added custom copper gutters and downspouts to ensure the roof's longevity. Because of those added details, that Galvalume roof made St. Roch Market look like an entirely new building.

At Juneau Odenwald Roofing, we make sure to give your new roof durability yet preserve the beauty of the historic building or home. Our team of roofing contractors likes to provide you with many options to renovate your landmark roof with metal roofing and other needed modifications.
