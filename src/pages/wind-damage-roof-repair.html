The Potential for Wind Damage

Given its subtropical climate, New Orleans sees serious storms on a regular basis. From summertime thunderstorms to hurricanes blowing in off the Gulf of Mexico, high winds are just something that New Orleans residents are forced to endure.

Those high winds pose a very real threat to your home and can lift, crumble and tear shingles and even blow off tiles. Once the shingles or tiles of your roof are damaged, it takes very little time for water to leak in and start causing rot, mold and mildew within your roof structure or attic. Wind can also hurl broken limbs and other debris into your roof, causing damage to shingles and tiles, as well as damage to the underlying structure of your roof.
