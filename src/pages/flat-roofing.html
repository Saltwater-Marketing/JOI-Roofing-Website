Modified Bitumen Roofing

Juneau Odenwald Roofing is located in New Orleans, Louisiana and has both the experience and expertise to install and maintain long-lasting and reliable flat roofs and help ensure that your roof will provide you with many years of watertight service.

Additionally, we have extensive experience with modified bitumen roofing systems. Modified bitumen is a type of asphalt with additives which provide plastic or rubber-like properties. Modified bitumen (MB) is supplied in rolls with a variety of surfaces including aluminum, an aggregate, or copper. The roof is torched down or hot mopped applied in one, two or three layers.

With quality installations, the average MB roof system lasts nine or ten years, although some may last as long as fifteen years.

Single Ply TPO

Just look around next time you drive across the Crescent City Connection or peek out the window in a downtown office buildings...you'll see the distinctive white color of TPO single ply roofs everywhere.

There's quite a few reasons TPO roofing membranes are so popular in metro New Orleans and the Gulf Coast.

* Cooling properties - generally speaking, it's hot here in New Orleans, and TPO roofs help reflect UV radiation, keeping the surface of the roof and the entire building cool when they need it most. This helps keep energy costs to a minimum.

* Sustainability - TPO is widely hailed as one of the most energy efficient surfaces you can put on a commercial building. The materials contain no toxic or hazardous materials and are fully recyclable.

* Durability - TPO roofs are highly resistant to tears and damage, yet are still flexible enough to allow for normal building movement which is especially important in high wind areas like New Orleans.

Built Up Roofing (BUR)

Traditional "tar and gravel" built up roofing (BUR) has been in use on commercial buildings with low slope roofs for more than 100 years. And with good reason. They provide excellent waterproofing and ultra violet protection. They are low maintenance and built to withstand rough weather which we often have here in metro New Orleans, giving you an overall low cost of maintenance.

Contact us today for a quote on Built Up roofing.

EPDM (Rubber Flat Roofs)

EPDM is another popular roof choice because it is extremely durable, available in black or white and comes in a variety of widths and thicknesses. There are also many ways it can be installed giving you great flexibility.

In particular, EPDM's resistance to wind damage and resistance to extreme heat has made it especially useful here in metro New Orleans for obvious reasons.

Advantages of a Flat Roof

Flat roofs offer advantages to those who choose them. Some of those advantages include:

  • Lower cost to maintain
  • Easily accessible
  • Spaced saved on home interior
  • Construction time saved
  • Facilitates installation of solar panels
  • Can be used as green turf roof when appropriate
  • Architecturally appealing

Flat roofs do require yearly inspections and while there is cost associated with that, any extra expenses are easily offset by the increase the longevity of your roof and savings on long-term maintenance. Flat roofs are easily re-coated.
