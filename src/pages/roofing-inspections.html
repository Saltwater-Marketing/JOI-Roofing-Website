Why Roofing Inspections are Important

Roofing inspections are very important in a variety of situations:

  • When buying a home or building — know what condition the roof is in before you make a purchase decision as the life expectancy of the roof could greatly impact the final price.
  • Spring inspections – roofs can take a serious beating during winter weather even in south Louisiana.
  • Fall inspections – it is also a good idea to get an inspection before the winter weather season.
  • After storms – storms are a way of life along the Gulf Coast, and in the event of severe weather you need to have your roof inspected to ensure any damages can be quickly repaired. Ask about our emergency roofing services.
  • Uncovering unseen damage — sometimes you can go a year with a damaged roof before you see that damage
  • Repair or Replace – oftentimes business and home owners are not sure if they should repair their roof or replace it. We can help you understand your choices and make an informed decision. You can also request a free estimate to get an idea of how much it would cost to replace your roof.

The most important reason to inspect your roof is to prevent damage in the first place and to prevent some small level of damage from getting worse and increasing your costs to repair.

Don’t wait until a little problem grows into a major roofing and interior costly repair job.

What You Can Expect

When we inspect your roof, you can expect a very detailed and accurate report on the current status of your roof and recommendations on action that you should take. This means we will look for any necessary repairs that must and should be made. We will generate a report than may include photographs, detailed notes, and an estimate of repair costs. We will provide detailed information that the home or building seller and buyer both need. Inspections will include our professional opinion needed by mortgage lenders, insurance adjusters and possibly roof riders on home warranty policies.
