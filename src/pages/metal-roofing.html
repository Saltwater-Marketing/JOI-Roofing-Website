The Undeniable Value of a Metal Roofing System

Today, you see more and more metal roof systems - from the traditional tin roof of the Texas and Louisiana countryside to the attractive architectural copper metal roofing in sophisticated urban settings. Metal roofs are also frequent features throughout our area’s industrial park complexes, attractively complimenting the metal building itself.

Metal roof installation offers significant value to the homeowner or building-owner. From simple cottages to large commercial or agricultural buildings, metal roofs almost always outlast rival roofing materials. They easily pay for themselves in their years of durability, energy efficiency, and eco-friendly features.

Unlike traditional asphalt shingles, metal roofs do not need to be replaced every ten to fifteen years on average. Metal roofing is among the most sustainable roofing materials available. Additionally, metal roofing does not crack, shrink, or erode with exposure to the elements. Sheet metal material is capable of withstanding storm-grade winds and driving rain, reflects heat, has impact-resistant properties, and sheds debris easily.

Plus, as a homeowner, you may be eligible for a discount on your homeowner’s insurance premium after installing a durable and sustainable sheet metal roof.

Key Advantages of Metal Roofing Systems in Lifespan, Maintenance, and Durability

Lifespan: One of the single most common reasons to invest in a metal roof for a home or business is to take advantage of the immense longevity on offer. Most asphalt shingle roofs last 20 to 25 years and then must be replaced. A metal roof, on the other hand, can last between 40 and 70 years depending on the type of metal chosen, the specific roofing system used, and the skill of the installation team. That more than doubles the lifespan of your roof in comparison with traditional options, perhaps even tripling your roof’s lifetime.

Maintenance: Metal roofs require very little in the way of maintenance. For instance, the only real maintenance you’ll need to do to the roof itself is periodic cleaning of dirt and mildew that might accumulate overtime. Of course, it is essential that you perform this task safely.

If you are unable to clean your roof safely, we highly recommend hiring a professional to handle the task for you. In addition to cleaning the metal roof panels themselves, you will also need to clean out the valley or other low-slope areas of the roof. The gutters must also be cleaned out frequently to prevent water from damming and backing up under the roofing and into your home.

Stability and Durability: You will find that metal roofs are incredibly stable and durable. In fact, depending on the material chosen, your roof will be able to withstand hurricane-strength winds of 140 miles per hour and even higher with the right roofing system. This is one reason that correctly installed metal roof systems are recommended by FEMA for use in high-wind regions, such as in Tornado Alley, and in the Gulf South states, where hurricane-force winds are a possibility. Depending on the thickness, or gauge, of your roof, this material is also incredibly impact-resistant and not susceptible to spark or lightning-induced fire hazards.

Eco-Friendly: If you’re interested in doing your part for the environment, metal roofing systems are environmentally friendly, especially when compared to traditional asphalt shingles.

Metal roofing has significantly lower levels of carbon emissions and pollution during their manufacture, and they can also help home and business owners reduce their electricity consumption by providing better temperature control. Sheet metal is also frequently made with up to 95% recycled content, making it an eco-friendly and green solution.

Not All Metal Roofs Are the Same

While we’re proud to offer business-owners and homeowners in Metro New Orleans access to beautiful, durable metal roofs, it’s important that you realize not all such roofs are created equal.

How do they differ?

That is one of the most common questions we receive from our customers. After all, one metal roof looks much like another, with exceptions for things like color. Actually, there are several key ways metal roofing can differ.

Standing Seam Metal Roofs: Take a look at your options and you will find a couple of different metal roofing systems available.

Standing seam style is one of your options. In this scenario, all of the seams (the joints between any separate pieces of metal) are raised above the lowest level of the roof itself. This is about more than mere looks. By raising seams higher, it helps keep those areas most likely to develop leaks drier. Standing seams are also raised, so there is no chance that the seam will be submerged during torrential rains, helping to ensure that no leaks develop overtime.

In comparison, flush-mount metal roofing puts all the seams at the same level - flush with the rest of the roof. Obviously, there is a greater chance that leaks will develop in this type of installation. Often, standing seam roofs are your best option.

Color Protection: At one point in time, metal roofs were only available in silver, galvanized metal. This really limited your choice! Today, that’s no longer the case. You can choose from a wide range of colors to match the aesthetic of your home or business. Color options include black and brown all the way to red and even multi-color variants such as verdigris, or patina.

Traditional roofing systems don’t generally offer the same amount of color protection as metal roofs do. We guarantee the colorfastness of your roof for a full 20 years, giving you peace of mind about your investment and ensuring its top-notch appearance for years to come.

Aesthetic Appeal: Take a look at your average metal roof in New Orleans and you’re likely to see dozens, even hundreds, of exposed screws. That’s natural. The sheet metal panels must be held in place, right? They do, but those screws don’t have to degrade your business or home’s aesthetic.

Our New Orleans roofing contractors install metal roofs so that all the screws necessary to hold clips in place are completely hidden from sight. This system offers significantly more aesthetic appeal for your home. We also custom-design ridge caps, gutters, and other elements to tie your roof to your home’s design and create a cohesive, attractive look.

Moisture Protection: Our metal roofing systems are significantly more watertight and moisture-repelling than traditional asphalt shingles. Our roof contractors’ process of tucking the securement screws out of sight ensures high moisture protection. As your roof heats and expands with heat and cold, exposed screw holes get larger and larger. Our roof systems have no exposed holes that might allow moisture to enter your home, keeping your structure safe and dry for decades to come.
