Roof Leak Repairs for Your Home or Business

A roof leak can happen to any roof for a variety of reasons. Clogged gutters, damaged sheathing or underlayment, and repetitive exposure to torrential rain, high-speed winds, and direct sunlight can all weaken a roof over the course of its useful life. Here in Southeastern Louisiana, we are also privy to the direct damage caused by violent hurricanes and occasional tornadoes.

As a result of destroyed or heavily damaged shingles, water can easily leak into homes and businesses, causing water stains which can lead to mold and mildew, jeopardizing the health of those nearby and compromising a building’s structure and integrity. Other signs of a leaky roof include rust, rotted bargeboard, fascia, or soffit, cracked flashing, and rotting plumbing vents and stacks.

Whether you are running a business or residing in a home with a defective roof, you know that water leaks are considered emergencies that cannot wait. If they are left unchecked, your roof can become further compromised and the interior walls and dormers will become seriously damaged. After all, if a roof collapses, a business may lose valuable stock or equipment and a homeowner could lose his or her prized possessions.

While you may have an inkling that you will save some money by trying some quick DIY repair like caulking the leak, you have to know that this is only a temporary fix and things will only get worse over time. You wouldn’t want to have to replace the entire roof of your home or business when the problem could have been remedied before it got out of hand. That is why we are always available to help you with your leaking roof or other pressing roofing problems. Give us a call and we’ll address any minor damage right away.

Commercial Roofing

When you are dealing with a large, flat roof or metal roof on a commercial building, you can’t ignore even the smallest leak. Doing so will only damage carpets, drywall, and any goods or equipment you have on your floor. After awhile, if the damage starts to add up, you may lose money by closing down tempaorarily to repair the roof, among other items that sustained damage. That why its is crucial to repair all roof leaks immediately.

At JOI Roofing, we also use preventative measures to keep commercial roofs from leaking when there are signs that a leak may occur. For example, flat roofs usually leak at low spots, forming what is referred to as “blisters.” They usually start out as simply water stains that may be missed initially.

That is why it is imperative that a commercial roofer is thorough when repairing a flat roof. This is especially true if the roof has a leak that has begun to blister, because the damaged area will need to removed and drained of all accumulated water before any major repairs can be made. This is done by slicing the blister straight through the middle with a utility knife and absorbing any water within it.

We then spread a viscous roofing cement thickly around the blister’s bottom edges as a sealant to ensure it’s watertight. That said, eventually, the material may give way and necessitate a more drastic solution. Remember, a quick fix helps in the short term, but the part of the roof that is in need of repair may continue to rot, causing further damage, and making it much harder to fix later on.
