Roof Damage Comes from Many Places

You’ll find that roof damage to your New Orleans home stems from numerous sources. Part of the problem is the environment in which we live – it’s a subtropical climate, which means lots of heat, heavy rains, strong winds and damaging hail are all common occurrences. The high humidity in New Orleans doesn’t help matters either.

Most roof damage starts with damaged shingles or tiles. Once that protective barrier has been compromised, water can easily penetrate beneath it. Repeated soakings will eventually warp and then rot the decking beneath your shingles, and then leak into the areas beneath the roof causing even more extensive damage.

This can lead to more than just rotted wood. Mold can easily form given the humid, dark conditions, and eventually become a health threat to you and your family. Juneau Odenwald Roofing can help ensure that water, rotting wood, mold and mildew do not ruin the value of your home or threaten your family.
