Why Is Roof Maintenance Necessary?

ou might not think it, but maintaining your roof properly is one of the most important considerations in order to ensure that it stays structurally sound and to detect any damage before it becomes a real problem.

In New Orleans, your roof is exposed to severe conditions on a daily basis. From dirt, dust and pollen to limbs, leaves and wind damage, myriad threats to your home’s safety and solidity exist. And of course, don’t forget the extreme heat and humidity we deal with for half of the year!

Even something as simple as leaves that fall on your roof can easily build up beneath eaves and within your gutters. Over time, those leaves trap moisture and allow mold to grow. Given insufficient attention, this can lead to rot and damage to your shingles, tiles or other roofing material. Leaves building up within your gutters will eventually cause the fascia to rot out, exposing the interior of your roof to the elements.

It’s not all about removing debris, though, and Juneau Odenwald Roofing understands that roof maintenance for New Orleans homes and businesses also requires other considerations.
