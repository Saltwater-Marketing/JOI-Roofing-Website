Project – Holy Name of Jesus Church / Loyola University

We at Holy Name could not be happier with the outstanding job completed by Juneau Odenwald, Inc. Almost immediately after the storm, they replaced the large pitched roof of the Church with composite slate leaving the Church with the best roof it has ever had in its almost 100 year history!

Then, they took the lateral flat roofs over the side aisles (roofs that had been over poured with creosote so many times that they resembled the Santa Monica tar pits) and replaced them with a tough, clean roofing material guaranteed for 20 years.

These flat roofs used to be like swimming pools after major rains. Now, with properly installed crickets and beautifully restored downspouts and flashing, the roofs drain completely after thunderstorms and there is no hint of a leak anywhere!

The people from Juneau Odenwald are our roofers of choice here at Loyola. They are faithful to their word and their promises. They really do set a high business standard which needs to be emulated by others.

A grateful Pastor,
Fr. Edwin L. Gros, SJ
Pastor of Holy Name of Jesus Church

Project – Gibbs Construction

Gibbs Construction has a long positive history with Juneau Odenwald. They have been reliable roofing and sheet metal subcontractors for Gibbs for more than 15 years.

In that time Juneau Odenwald has demonstrated expertise in the roof industry and they provided Gibbs with the highest quality roof systems, even under difficult job conditions and aggressive schedules.

Their assistance with pre-construction phases and scheduling provides Gibbs Construction with valuable insight which helps us deliver quality end products on time and in budget. Juneau Odenwald is professional, reliable and provides excellent services before, during and after our project.

Juneau Odenwald, Inc. is a roofing contractor built on a foundation of integrity, professionalism and high capability. They are client-centered and quality focused, and have evolved into a substantial asset to both Gibbs Construction and our valued clients.

Lawrence C. Gibbs
